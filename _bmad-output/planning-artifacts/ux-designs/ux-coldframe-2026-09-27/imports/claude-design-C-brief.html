COLDFRAME · DIRECTION C OF 3
Brief
The app opens with a sentence, like a note from a neighbour: "Tomatoes and Cucumbers need water. Beans has gone quiet." Lots are grouped under their status word, and moisture is drawn as a soft ±5 % range on a dry→wet scale, not a precise number.
Range marker: where the soil is, ±5 %, over the Threshold band
Unknown: the scale itself goes dotted. No marker is drawn
Stale: markers become hollow outlines, and the sentence switches to past tense
Colour is secondary here: status is carried by the section a Lot sits in and by plain words. The brand gradient is kept for moments of arrival: sign-in, lock screen, "Hub is online".
